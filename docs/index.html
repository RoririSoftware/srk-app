01What the app does

SKR sells farm machines. Most of them are sold under a government subsidy scheme. So every sale needs a file, and the department has to accept that file.

Right now the file is made by hand. Someone types the farmer's name, land details and survey numbers from photos of documents. If one thing is typed wrong, or one document says the village name differently, the department sends the file back. Fixing it takes about a week.

This app does three things.

1 · Reads the documents

Upload a photo of an Aadhaar card, a Patta or a certificate. The app reads it and fills the form. No typing.

2 · Checks the file

It compares every document against every other one. If something does not match, it shows you before you send the file.

3 · Runs the whole job

Lead, documents, product, delivery, service, stock and money. All in one place, for the whole team.

And the owner sees it all

Every lead, every branch, every staff member. Who did what, and when. From the web portal, live.

03Photo instead of typing

This is the main idea of the app. Everything else is built around it.

  1. Take a photo, or pick one from the phone. A scan, a screenshot or a camera photo all work.
  2. The app reads it. It knows which document it is looking at, and pulls out every field.
  3. You check what it read. The photo is on one side, the fields on the other. Fix anything wrong.
  4. Save. The fields go into the farmer's record. The form is already filled in.

It reads Tamil documents too - land type, village name, taluk. It also converts hectare-are into acres on its own, because that is where people make mistakes.

One app, no WhatsApp

Everything happens inside the app. Staff upload the photo there, see what was read, and fix it there. Nothing is sent to another number and nothing is typed twice.

04What the app reads

Nine documents. Each one fills different fields.

DocumentWhat the app pulls out of it
Aadhaar cardName, father or husband name, date of birth, gender, address, Aadhaar number
Farmer registrationThe farmer's registration ID
Patta / ChittaPatta number, survey number, sub division, land type, extent, water rate, district, taluk, village
AdangalCrop and cultivation details against the survey number
SMF certificateCertificate number and date, farmer name, father name, address, every land row, total extent
Community certificateCommunity. If it is not uploaded, the farmer is taken as General
RC bookVehicle number and registration date
Bank passbookAccount number, IFSC, name on the account
Farmer photoJust stored with the file

Patta and SMF certificate already work today. The rest are built the same way.

A document can come in as a photo or as a PDF. Staff often already have the PDF on the phone, so making them photograph a screen would be a step backwards.

Aadhaar needs a decision

Keeping full Aadhaar numbers and card photos has legal rules in India. The app can read the card to fill the form and then keep only a masked number, or keep the full number. SKR should choose. See question 06.

05What the app checks

Before a file is sent, the app runs these checks. It shows one report at the end. Green means the file is ready.

  • Name. Same name on Aadhaar, Patta, certificate, passbook and the registration record. Titles like Thiru and Thirumathi are ignored, and so is word order.
  • Village, taluk and district. Same place written the same way on every document. The app keeps a list of villages that are known by two names, so a real mistake stands out.
  • Patta and survey numbers. Same numbers on the certificate and the Patta. Order does not matter.
  • Land extent. Hectare-are changed to acres, rows added up, and compared with the total.
  • Certificate date. Flags a certificate that is too old to use.
  • Community. Taken from the community certificate if there is one. If not, General.
  • Vehicle age. Registration date from the RC book, checked against the age limit.
  • Product and brand. Is this brand approved for this department and this scheme?
  • Missing documents. Which documents does this scheme need, and which are still not uploaded?

If something does not match, the app names the field and shows both values side by side. You fix it and run the check again. The report can be saved as a PDF.

Delivery

Money

Reports

  • Leads by stage, department, branch and staff
