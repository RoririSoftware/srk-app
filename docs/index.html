01What we are building

SKR sells agricultural machinery. Most of that machinery is bought under a government subsidy scheme, which means every sale is really two jobs running at once: selling a machine, and assembling a file the department will accept.

The second job is where the money leaks. A file is submitted, a village name is spelled one way on the Patta and another way in the farmer database, and the application comes back. Correcting it on the government portal takes up to seven days. Multiply that across a season and it is weeks of lost time, a frustrated farmer, and sometimes a lost sale.

This system does three things about that.

1 · Collect without typing

A photograph of an Aadhaar card, a Patta, a certificate - sent from the field or over WhatsApp - is read automatically and lands in the right field, against the right farmer.

2 · Check before the department does

Every document is compared against every other one, and against what the scheme requires, before anything is submitted. Mismatches surface while the farmer is still standing there.

3 · Carry the file to the end

One record moves from telecaller to coordinator to store to delivery to accounts, with a timeline showing who did what. Nothing is skipped and nothing is re-typed.

And underneath: control

The owner decides who can see prices, who can approve, who can delete. Everyone else sees only the slice of the business they are responsible for.

02The business behind it

SMAM, and the three departments under it

SMAM is the central scheme under which subsidised machinery is sold. Applications do not all go to one office - they are routed to one of three implementing departments, and each has its own product list, its own subsidy rates, its own approved brands and its own application window.

SMAM │ ├── AED Agricultural Engineering Department │ tractors, power tillers, heavy implements │ ├── Agri Department of Agriculture │ general farm machinery, small implements │ └── Horti Department of Horticulture horticulture equipment · window: August → March

So a lead is never just "farmer plus product". It is farmer, product, department and scheme year. The same power weeder applied for through Agri and through AED can carry a different subsidy percentage, a different approved brand list and a different document checklist. The app has to know which department a file belongs to from the moment the product is chosen.

The registration ID changed everything

The old process was paper: submit the documents, and it was done. That is over. Every farmer now has a registration ID, and the department reads its own database off that ID. Without the ID, nothing moves. And if any detail on the submitted documents disagrees with what that database already holds, the file stops.

This is the single most important fact in this document, and it shapes the whole system: the farmer's registration ID is the spine. Every document, every land record, every order and every delivery hangs off it. One farmer, one ID, one record - so that what SKR submits and what the department already believes are the same thing.

Why files come back

  • Name spelled differently across Aadhaar, Patta, certificate and the department database - including honorifics and reordered words.
  • Village or taluk written two ways. One document says Mukoodal, another says Idaikaal, and the portal treats them as different places.
  • Land extent that does not add up once hectare-are notation is converted to acres.
  • A vehicle record that is too old. Machinery registered beyond roughly ten years is commonly rejected outright - and there is no point building a file around it.
  • A missing document that this particular scheme and this particular category required.

None of these need a human to find. All of them are currently found by the department, a week too late.

Not yet understood - needs SKR

The notes show, under AED: 1 to 7 → 6 (Lot), with circled figures 8, 22 and 32. We have read these as something like application categories 1-7, a lot or batch of 6, and counts against a total. We have deliberately not guessed further - Questions 14-19 ask SKR to explain this, because it likely defines how AED files are batched and submitted.

03How a file starts

Two doors, one record. Whichever door a document comes in through, it ends up attached to the same farmer, under the same registration ID.

Door 1 · WhatsApp

Staff send a photograph of an Aadhaar card to a dedicated SKR number. The system reads it, creates or matches the farmer, and replies with what it captured and what is still missing. The Patta, the community certificate, the RC book and the bank passbook follow the same way - each one recognised for what it is and filed into the right fields.

The appeal is obvious: no app to install, no training, and it is where the documents already are.

Door 2 · The app

The same thing with a camera and a form. Staff in the field open the attachments screen, photograph each document in turn, review what was read, correct anything wrong, and move on through the lead. Drafts survive a dropped signal.

This is the door that also handles everything WhatsApp cannot: approvals, stock, delivery, accounts and reporting.

A decision to make together

WhatsApp is a genuinely good intake channel, but it is not free and not unconstrained: business messaging runs through Meta's official API, is charged per conversation, and can only reply to a person within a set window after they message. It is also a poor place to review a fifty-field form. Our recommendation is to build the app first and add WhatsApp intake as a second channel into the same system, rather than trying to run the whole business through chat. Question 09 puts this to SKR directly.

Two of these already work today: the Patta reader and the certificate reader, including Tamil land-type text, hectare-to-acre conversion and multi-row certificates. The rest follow the same pattern.

Aadhaar needs a decision, not an assumption

Storing full Aadhaar numbers and card images carries legal obligations in India around consent, masking and retention. We can read an Aadhaar to fill the form and then store only a masked number, or store the full number if SKR's process genuinely requires it. This is a choice SKR should make deliberately, with its own advice - Question 27.

05Catching rejections early

This is the part that pays for the system. Before anything is submitted, every document is checked against every other document, and against what the scheme demands. The result is one pre-submission report - and the file only goes out when it is clean.

  1. Name, across everythingAadhaar, Patta, certificate, bank passbook and the registration record compared as a set of words, with honorifics stripped and initials ignored, so a real difference stands out and a harmless one does not.
  2. Place names, across everythingDistrict, taluk and village compared across documents, with a maintained list of known alternate spellings so that two names for the same village are recognised rather than flagged - and two genuinely different villages are caught.
  3. Patta and survey numbersCompared as sets, so order and punctuation do not matter, and a missing survey number is obvious.
  4. Land extentHectare-are notation converted to acres, row totals added up and compared against the stated total, within a tolerance that allows for rounding but not for a real discrepancy.
  5. Certificate validityThe Small & Marginal Farmer certificate checked against its issue date and flagged if it falls outside the accepted window.
  6. Category and communityIf a community certificate is attached, the category is taken from it; if not, the farmer is treated as General - and the subsidy slab follows from that automatically.
  7. Vehicle ageRegistration date read from the RC book and checked against the age limit before effort is spent on the file.
  8. Scheme eligibilityIs this product, this brand and this category actually approved for this department and this scheme year? Checked at the point the product is chosen, not after submission.
  9. CompletenessWhich documents does this scheme, for this category, actually require - and which are still missing? Shown as a checklist that has to be green before the file can be submitted.

The output is a single report: every field, what each document says, and a verdict. Clean files are marked ready. Anything doubtful is put in front of a person, with the exact disagreement named. The report can be downloaded as a PDF for the file.

06Who uses the app

Three levels of access.

SUPER ADMIN (Owner) │ sees everything · creates every account · the only role that can delete │ ├── ADMIN - department heads, full control of their own area │ 1. Coordinator runs the pipeline, approves files │ 2. Store stock, allocation, serial numbers │ 3. Delivery dispatch, transport, proof of delivery │ 4. Accounts invoices, subsidy claims, payments │ └── STAFF - day-to-day operators, narrow permissions 1. Telecaller / Sales creates and chases leads 2. Department Coordinator handles one department's filings

The important line is the one between Admin and Staff. An admin can approve, reassign and edit within their department. Staff can only create and update their own work - they cannot approve their own files, and they cannot see the whole pipeline.

07Role by role

Super Admin - the owner

Opens the app to: the whole business on one screen. Leads by stage, by department, by branch, by staff member. Money committed, subsidy claimed, subsidy received, stock on hand, rejection rate.

Can do: everything. Create and deactivate accounts, assign roles, set subsidy rates and prices, maintain approved brand and product lists, override any stage, delete records, export everything, read the full history.

Only role that can: delete a record, and see cost price and margin.

Coordinator - admin

Opens the app to: an approval queue - files that passed the checks and are waiting for a human to say "send it".

Can do: approve or reject with a reason, reassign a lead between staff, set priority, push an approved file onward, chase anything stuck.

Cannot: delete records, change prices, or create accounts.

Store - admin

Opens the app to: approved leads waiting for stock, and the current stock position by product and branch.

Can do: receive stock in, allocate a specific unit by serial, chassis and engine number, mark it ready for dispatch, flag low stock, run a physical count.

Cannot: see farmer financial details, approve files, or change the scheme.

Delivery - admin

Opens the app to: today's run sheet - which machines go to which villages, in what order.

Can do: assign vehicle and driver, mark dispatched, capture proof of delivery with photo, signature and date, record a failed or postponed delivery with a reason.

Cannot: edit farmer or land details, see prices, or close a lead.

Accounts - admin

Opens the app to: delivered leads that are not yet financially closed.

Can do: raise the invoice, record the farmer's contribution, file the subsidy claim, record subsidy received, flag a shortfall, close the lead.

Cannot: alter documents or delivery records - they read them as evidence for the claim.

Telecaller / Sales - staff

Opens the app to: their own farmers and today's call list.

Can do: create a lead, log a call outcome and a follow-up date, send or photograph the documents, review what was read, see the check report, and submit for approval.

Cannot: see other staff members' leads, approve anything, see cost price, or move a file past "submitted".

Department Coordinator - staff

Opens the app to: the queue for one department only - AED, Agri or Horti.

Can do: take approved files, batch them for submission, record the department's reference number and status, and attach the sanction order.

Cannot: see other departments' files, or change product and price.

09Life of one lead

Ten stages. Each is owned by exactly one role, and a file cannot skip forward.

  1. Lead createdTelecaller

    Farmer captured from an Aadhaar photograph, matched to a registration ID, and completed through the wizard: basic details, land, product and scheme, delivery.

  2. Documents uploadedTelecaller

    Patta, certificate, community certificate, RC book and passbook sent in from the app or over WhatsApp, read automatically and confirmed field by field by the person holding the phone.

  3. Documents verifiedSystem, then Coordinator

    The nine checks in Section 05 run and produce one report. A clean file is marked ready; a disagreement is put in front of a person with the exact field named.

  4. File approvedCoordinator

    A human signs off that the file is fit to submit. Rejections go back to the telecaller with a reason.

  5. Submitted to departmentDept Coordinator

    Batched and filed with AED, Agri or Horti. Reference number recorded, status tracked until sanctioned or rejected.

  6. Product confirmed, stock allocatedStore

    A specific physical unit - serial, chassis, engine number - is tied to the file and taken out of available stock.

  7. DispatchedDelivery

    Vehicle and driver assigned, machine leaves the yard.

  8. Delivery completedDelivery

    Proof captured at the farmer's site: photo, signature, date.

  9. Warranty active, service scheduledService

    The warranty clock starts from the delivery date. Service type, engineer and next service due are tracked from here on.

  10. ClosedAccounts

    Invoice raised, farmer contribution received, subsidy claimed and received. Only then does the file close.

An ordering question we cannot answer ourselves

We have put department submission before stock allocation - wait for sanction, then commit a machine. If SKR actually delivers first and claims afterwards, stages 5 and 6-8 swap and the store screens change shape. Question 22.

11Build order

Four phases. Each ends with something SKR can actually put in a staff member's hands.

Phase 1 - Capture and check

Real accounts and login, the three access levels and the permission matrix, the lead wizard, the attachments screen, readers for Aadhaar, Patta, certificate, community certificate and RC book, the registration ID as the spine, and the pre-submission check report. Drafts that survive a dead signal.

Ends with: a telecaller able to build a complete, checked file in the field, and know before submitting whether it will pass.

Phase 2 - The office half

Coordinator approval queue. Department submission and batching. Store allocation with serial numbers. Delivery run sheet and proof of delivery. Warranty and service tracking.

Ends with: a file travelling from first call to delivered machine entirely inside the system.

Phase 3 - Money, reporting and WhatsApp

Invoicing, farmer contribution, subsidy claim tracking, reconciliation. Owner dashboard including rejection rate. Exports in the formats the departments expect. WhatsApp intake as a second door into the same records.

Ends with: the owner able to answer "how much subsidy are we owed, and why did those four files come back" without asking anyone.

Phase 4 - Sharpen

Better reading accuracy on worn and faded documents, Tamil interface, farmer notifications, service follow-up reminders, and any direct integration with government systems that turns out to be available.

Not estimating yet

Timelines and cost come after Section 12 is answered. Several answers - especially 09, 14-19 and 22 - change the size of Phase 1 and Phase 2 significantly, and we would rather quote once, accurately.

12Questions for SKR

Everything below is something we have had to assume. Correcting an assumption now costs a sentence; correcting it after Phase 2 costs weeks.

First, the obvious one

Is the business name SKR Trader or SRK Trader? Both spellings are in circulation between the artwork and the working files, and it needs to be right everywhere before anything is printed.

The registration ID and the government systems

Where does a farmer's registration ID come from - does the farmer already have one, or does SKR create it for them?Everything in the system hangs off this ID, so we need to know exactly how it is obtained.
Can SKR look up a farmer's existing registration details by ID, and if so, on which portal?
Is there any official way to read that data programmatically, or is it always a person looking at a screen?
What happens today when a farmer has no registration ID at all? Is that a lead you decline, or a step you do for them?
On e-Sevai, is the application typed in by hand every time? If so, would an export in exactly the right order and format save real time?
Roughly what share of files come back rejected in a year, and what are the top three reasons?This number is the business case. We would like to be able to show it falling.
Do you keep a list of villages that are written more than one way? If not, we will build one as we go - but an existing list would save months.

WhatsApp

Should WhatsApp intake be built in the first release, or added once the app is running?Our recommendation is the app first, WhatsApp second. This is the biggest scope question in the document.
Who would send documents to the WhatsApp number - only SKR staff, or farmers themselves?If farmers send directly, the system has to handle strangers, wrong documents and unreadable photos, which is a materially larger job.
Is there an existing SKR business number for this, and roughly how many documents a day would come through it?
Should the system reply on WhatsApp with what it read and what is missing, or stay silent and just file things?
Would SKR accept the same intake over a simple web link instead, if WhatsApp turns out to be expensive or restricted?

The scheme, the departments, and that page of numbers

Under AED the note reads "1 to 7". What are 1 to 7 - categories, stages of the application, or priority levels?Questions 14-19 all refer to the second half of the handwritten notes.
What does the circled 6 (Lot) mean? Are files submitted in lots of six?
What are the circled 8, 22 and 32 - counts, targets, quotas, or product codes?
Is there an allocation or quota per department per season that the system should count down against?
When files go to a department, do they go one at a time or as a batch under one covering reference?
What statuses does an application pass through, in the department's own words?
Do AED, Agri and Horti each have different approved product and brand lists, and different subsidy percentages? Can we have the current lists?
Horti runs August to March. What are the windows for AED and Agri?

Process and stages

Does SKR deliver the machine before or after the department sanctions the subsidy?This is the single biggest structural question in the document.
Is the ten-stage flow in Section 09 how the business actually runs? Anything missing, anything that never happens?
Can a file be cancelled after stock is allocated? Who may do that, and what happens to the reserved machine?
Roughly how many leads a month, and how many close?
Are staff paid any incentive per closed file? If so, the system needs to compute it.

Documents and checking

How should Aadhaar be stored - full number, masked number, or the image only until the file closes?This has legal implications and SKR should decide it deliberately.
The vehicle age limit: is it exactly ten years, and is it counted from the registration date?
Does the document checklist change by department, by category, or by product? We have assumed all three.
If every check passes cleanly, may the system approve on its own, or must a coordinator always press approve?
How much land extent difference between documents is genuinely acceptable before it is a real problem?
If a community certificate is missing, treating the farmer as General is our assumption. Correct?
How long must document photographs be kept after a file closes?

Stock, delivery, service and money

Is stock tracked per individual machine with a serial or chassis number, or only as a count per product?
Should the system print the invoice, or does that stay in Tally or whatever you use today?
If you already use accounting software, should the system push data into it, or will accounts re-enter?
Does the farmer pay in instalments, and does the system need to track a part-payment schedule?
What does a delivery need as proof to satisfy an audit - photo, signature, GPS location, all three?
Who are the service engineers, and should they get their own login and job list?

People and practicalities

Roughly how many people will use the system, and how do they split across the roles?
One branch or several? If several, should a person only ever see their own branch?
Can one person hold two roles - a store keeper who also does deliveries? We have assumed yes.
Android only, or iPhones too? And does anyone need to work on a laptop rather than a phone?
How bad is the network in the villages your staff work in? How much must work fully offline?
Should the interface be in Tamil, English, or both with a switch?
Do farmers get any notification when their file is sanctioned or their delivery is scheduled?
Is there existing data - a sheet of farmers, current stock - that must be imported on day one?
Which reports do you produce today, for whom, and in what format? Those are the exports we must match exactly.
Is there a deadline - a season opening, a scheme year - that the first release has to be ready for?
